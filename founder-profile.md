# Founder Profile

Stable information about the founder, gathered once and reused across all ventures. Update this
file whenever your situation changes — the studio should never make you re-explain it per venture.

**Anything you haven't answered should read `Unknown — requires input.`, not a guessed default.**
That phrasing is load-bearing: it tells the orchestrator to either ask you or make an explicitly
labeled assumption, instead of quietly inventing a constraint and optimizing against it.

You do not have to fill this in by hand. Run `/new-venture` and the orchestrator will ask you the
3-5 questions that actually matter, then write the answers here.

## Skills & Background

- Technical capability: Unknown — requires input.
  _(Do you write and ship code yourself? Rely on AI-assisted development? Hire contractors? This
  single answer changes how aggressively the Build vs. Buy Rule — CLAUDE.md §9 — gets applied.)_
- Industry knowledge / domain expertise: Unknown — requires input.
  _(Be specific about depth. "Finance" and "German tax practice" are very different advantages.
  A real domain edge is one of the few durable ones a solo founder can have — and claiming one you
  don't have is a fast way to mis-score an idea.)_
- Sales capability: Unknown — requires input.
- Prior startup / business experience: Unknown — requires input.

## Resources

- Available capital: Unknown — requires input.
  _(How much can you spend before the venture must pay for itself? This caps which ideas are even
  reachable — a regulated fintech build is not a €2,000 project.)_
- Available time (hours/week, full-time vs. side project): Unknown — requires input.
  _(Be honest here rather than aspirational. Time is the constraint that most often invalidates an
  otherwise good idea, especially for channels that are paid for in hours rather than euros.)_

## Preferences

- Desired business model (SaaS, services-as-software, marketplace, etc.): Unknown — requires input.
- B2B vs. B2C preference: Unknown — requires input.
  _(Note whether this is a hard rule or a soft lean — the orchestrator will treat it differently.)_
- Geographic preference / target markets: Unknown — requires input.
  _(Worth stating even if you think it doesn't matter. Local market knowledge, language, regulation
  and payment habits routinely decide whether a solo founder can win a niche at all.)_
- Revenue objective: Unknown — requires input.
  _(e.g. "€10K MRR in 12 months", "lifestyle business", "VC-scale". State a number and a date if
  you can — it's what lets the business-analyst agent tell you when the arithmetic doesn't work.)_

## Constraints

- Unacceptable constraints (industries, business models, workloads you will not accept):
  Unknown — requires input.
  _(This is the most valuable field in the file and the most commonly left blank. Ruling things out
  up front — "no cold calling", "I won't become a content creator", "nothing requiring 24/7
  support" — saves the studio from researching ideas you'd never execute. Say whether each is a
  hard rule or open to a per-venture exception.)_

- **Derived constraints:** _(the orchestrator fills this in — the compounded consequences of the
  answers above, e.g. "side-project hours + no personal coding + a hard €10K MRR target means
  weight heavily toward low technical complexity, fast time to MVP, and distribution that does not
  depend on audience-building")_

## Notes

Free-form notes you or the orchestrator want kept in view — "prefers async work", "cannot do
outbound cold calling", "has an existing audience in X niche", a new domain you've become curious
about. Date these entries; interests shift, and a stale note read as current is worse than no note.
