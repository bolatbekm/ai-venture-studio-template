# Decision Log

> Append-only. Never edit or delete a past entry — if a decision is reversed, add a new entry
> that references it. This is what prevents circular discussions and forgotten reasoning.
> Use `/log-decision` to append correctly, or follow the template below manually.

## Template

```
### YYYY-MM-DD — <short decision title>

- **DECISION:** What was decided.
- **WHY:** The reasoning.
- **EVIDENCE:** What supported it (with sources where applicable).
- **ASSUMPTIONS:** What we're still assuming, unverified.
- **ALTERNATIVES CONSIDERED:** What else was on the table, and why it lost.
- **REVERSIBLE / IRREVERSIBLE:** Which, and why.
- **WHEN TO REVISIT:** A trigger or date, not "eventually."
```
